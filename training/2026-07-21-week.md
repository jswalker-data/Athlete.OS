### Strength sessions in full

**Wed Jul 22 — Session A: Glute & hip stability**
1. Glute bridge — 3×12, 2s pause at the top. *Squeeze glutes, not lower back.*
2. Single-leg glute bridge — 2×10 each side. *Keep hips level, don't let the free side drop.*
3. Clamshells — 2×15 each side. *Slow, controlled, no rocking the pelvis.*
4. Side-lying leg raises — 2×12 each side. *Leg stays in line with the body.*
5. Standing single-leg balance — 2×30s each side. *Boring on purpose — builds the stability that protects the hip under fatigue.*
6. Side plank — 2×20-30s each side. *Hips lifted, straight line shoulder-to-ankle.*

**Fri Jul 24 — Session B: Quad/patellar control, calf, core**
1. Spanish squat or wall sit — 3×30-40s. *Knees tracking over toes, weight through the heels — the patellar-tendon builder, don't rush it.*
2. Step-downs (off a low step/stair) — 2×10 each side, slow. *Control the descent — eccentric control is what actually protects the knee on downhills.*
3. Single-leg calf raise — 3×12 each side. *Full range, pause at the top.*
4. Bird-dog — 2×10 each side. *Opposite arm/leg, keep hips square.*
5. Dead bug — 2×10 each side. *Lower back stays flat on the floor throughout.*
6. Standing hip flexor stretch + calf stretch — 30s each side, to close out.
